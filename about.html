About MortgageCalc US

MortgageCalc US is an independent educational website providing simple mortgage calculation tools and explanatory guides for US homebuyers.

Our approach

We show assumptions clearly and separate principal and interest from taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA costs. Results are estimates, not lender quotes.

How the site is maintained

Calculator formulas and explanatory content are reviewed as part of site updates. Where practical, we link to primary consumer resources so visitors can verify important mortgage concepts and requirements. The calculators are designed for scenario planning rather than personalized financial recommendations.

Not financial advice

Use the calculator for education and scenario planning. Confirm rates, fees, taxes, insurance and loan terms with qualified professionals and official lender documents.
