# Market research — US mortgage calculator

Research date: September 29, 2026.

## Competitive patterns observed
- Zillow's calculator combines home price, down payment, loan program and rate with advanced costs and links to related mortgage calculators.
- NerdWallet exposes home price, down payment, rate, term and advanced property-tax, insurance, HOA and PMI inputs, plus loan totals and amortization.
- Bankrate uses home price, down payment, credit-score range and ZIP code, and explains that taxes, insurance, HOA and PMI can materially affect the payment.
- CFPB explains that total mortgage payment can include principal, interest, mortgage insurance and escrow for taxes and homeowners insurance.

## Product gap to target
Large competitors already own broad mortgage-calculator intent. A small new site should not win by copying their UI or publishing dozens of thin pages. The approach is a fast calculator plus transparent assumptions, useful amortization explanations, scenario comparisons and focused supporting content.

## SEO architecture
Core: /
Supporting intent: /guides/how-mortgage-payments-work.html, /guides/mortgage-amortization-explained.html, /guides/pmi-explained.html
Trust: /about.html, /privacy.html, /terms.html, /contact.html

## Monetization
Reserve ad inventory now, but add actual advertising only after the site is complete, policy-compliant and has useful content. Future monetization can include AdSense and direct mortgage/real-estate sponsorships, subject to applicable policies and disclosures.
