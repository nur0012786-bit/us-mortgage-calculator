PMI Explained

Private mortgage insurance, commonly called PMI, can increase the monthly cost of a conventional mortgage when the borrower has a relatively small down payment. The exact requirement and pricing depend on the loan and borrower.

Why it matters in a calculator

Looking only at principal and interest can understate the monthly housing cost. The calculator therefore lets you model PMI separately and removes the estimate when the down payment reaches 20% of the purchase price.

Do not treat the estimate as a quote

PMI pricing can depend on credit, loan-to-value, loan type and other factors. Confirm the actual premium with the lender.
