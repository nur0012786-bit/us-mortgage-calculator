How Mortgage Payments Work

A US mortgage payment can contain several components. Principal is the amount borrowed. Interest is the lender's charge for the loan. Property taxes and homeowners insurance may be collected through escrow, while PMI may apply depending on the loan and down payment.

Principal and interest

For a fixed-rate mortgage, the monthly principal-and-interest payment is based on the loan amount, interest rate and term. Early payments generally contain more interest; later payments contain more principal as the balance falls.

Why the total payment is higher

The CFPB explains that a total monthly mortgage payment can include principal, interest, mortgage insurance and escrow for property taxes and homeowners insurance. HOA dues may be separate.
