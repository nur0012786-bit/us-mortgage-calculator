HOME BUYING COST GUIDE

Closing Costs Explained for Home Buyers

Closing costs are the upfront costs involved in getting the mortgage and completing the home purchase. They are separate from the down payment.

What are closing costs?

Closing costs can include lender charges, title and settlement services, government recording charges, prepaid items and initial escrow funding. The exact mix depends on the transaction, location, loan and services selected.

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau describes closing costs as upfront costs charged to get the loan and transfer ownership. Your Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure are the documents to use when reviewing the actual figures.

Down payment versus closing costs

Your down payment reduces the amount borrowed. Closing costs pay for expenses associated with the transaction and loan. You therefore need to plan for both when estimating the cash required to buy a home.

What is cash to close?

Cash to close is the amount you need to bring to closing after accounting for applicable credits, deposits and other amounts already paid. It is not always equal to down payment plus a simple percentage because the actual transaction includes many line items.

How to use an estimate responsibly

Our closing cost calculator uses a percentage-based planning assumption. It is intentionally simple. When you receive a Loan Estimate, replace the estimate with the lender's actual figures and investigate differences rather than relying on a generic percentage.

Seller credits and lender credits

Credits can reduce some costs, but they are not free money. The applicable rules and the way credits interact with pricing and closing costs depend on the transaction and loan. Compare the actual Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure.

Closing-cost planning checklist

Down payment

Cash used toward the home's purchase price.

Lender costs

Origination and other charges associated with obtaining the loan.

Settlement costs

Title, recording and other transaction-related services.

Prepaids & escrow

Items such as prepaid interest and initial escrow funding may affect cash to close.
