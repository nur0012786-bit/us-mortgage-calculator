How we approach mortgage content

Mortgage calculations can look simple while the assumptions behind them are not. Our guides separate the loan calculation from taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance and other housing costs, explain important limitations, and point readers to consumer resources when a lender document or loan-specific rule should control.

For official consumer guidance, see the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's homebuying resources.
