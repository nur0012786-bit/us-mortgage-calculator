What this mortgage calculator includes

The calculator separates principal and interest from common escrow and housing costs. Your loan amount is the home price minus the down payment. This matters because the total payment can be materially higher than principal and interest alone. The CFPB notes that property taxes, homeowners insurance and mortgage insurance can be part of the total monthly payment. Learn more from the CFPB.

How the payment is calculated

For a fixed-rate loan, the principal-and-interest payment uses the standard amortization formula. Taxes, insurance and HOA are added separately. PMI is estimated while the modeled balance is above 80% of the original home value. Extra principal payments reduce the modeled payoff time and interest.

How we calculate your estimate

1. Loan amount

We subtract the down payment from the home price to estimate the amount financed.

2. Principal & interest

For a fixed-rate loan, we use the standard monthly amortization formula based on the loan amount, rate and term.

3. Housing costs

Property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA are modeled separately so you can see what contributes to the estimated total.

4. Extra payments

Optional extra principal is applied to the balance each month to estimate potential interest and payoff-time savings.

These are planning estimates, not lender quotes. For additional consumer guidance, see the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau mortgage resources.

Mortgage calculator FAQ

Does this calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes. The advanced inputs let you estimate property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA costs separately from principal and interest.

Why is my total payment higher than principal and interest?

Principal and interest are only part of the housing payment. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA dues can add to the monthly cost.

Does PMI automatically disappear at 20% equity?

Not necessarily. This calculator uses a simplified 80% balance threshold for its estimate. Actual PMI cancellation rules depend on the loan, lender and applicable requirements.

What happens if I pay extra each month?

Extra principal can reduce the balance faster and may reduce total interest. The calculator compares the standard schedule with the extra-payment scenario you enter.

Is this calculator a lender quote?

No. It is an educational planning tool. Actual rates, fees, taxes, insurance, PMI and lender underwriting can differ.
