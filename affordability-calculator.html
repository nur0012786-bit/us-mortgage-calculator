How affordability is estimated

This tool starts with gross monthly income and a target total debt-to-income ratio, subtracts existing monthly debt payments, then estimates the housing payment that remains. It uses the selected rate and term to solve for an approximate loan amount and adds the down payment.

Why this is only an estimate

Actual approval depends on the lender, credit profile, loan program, property, reserves and verified income and debts. Use the result as a planning range rather than a promise of what you can borrow.
